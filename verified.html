✓ Verified by Attelkom

The green tick means an Attelkom office has seen the business licence (or patent) of the business and the passport of the person who manages the page. It does not mean Attelkom recommends the business.

How to get it

  1. Claim your page or add your business online.
  2. Within 30 days come to an Attelkom office with the licence or patent, your passport and the application number.
  3. The office checks the documents. The tick appears within 5 working days.

Verification is free. Businesses on Attelkom Business Hosting are verified when they sign the hosting contract.
