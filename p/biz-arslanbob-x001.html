About

Guesthouse No. 17 in the walnut groves above the Arslanbob-Ata shrine, since 2002. Five rooms. Bed, breakfast and dinner. Horses to the falls, a guide to the high lakes. Licensed by the Arslanbob village council for guests from abroad: bring your passport, every guest is written in the council's book.
