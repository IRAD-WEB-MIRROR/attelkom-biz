About

Altyn-Bulak, the golden spring: naturally carbonated, iron-rich mineral water from the springs by Altyn-Bulak village near Iyık Janyl, bottled since 1978. About 6 million litres a year, in the green 0.5 l glass bottle and, since 2004, in 1.5 l.

«Алтын-Булак», природная газированная железистая минеральная вода. Разливается с 1978 года.

Our history

  1. 1971Listed in the Mineral Waters and Resorts of the Iradi SSR.
  2. 1978The bottling plant opens.
  3. 1996Privatised.
  4. 2004The 1.5 litre bottle.
