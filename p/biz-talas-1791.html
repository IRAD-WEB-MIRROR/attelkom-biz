About

Old City Brewing, a craft brewery and taproom in a former workshop in the Köhne Diz, since 2018. Four beers named for the Old City: Citadel Pale Ale, Temir-Kapka Stout, Kalan Darwaza Amber and Shahr-i Kabud Wheat. Sixty seats; kegs for bars on request.
