About

Chaikhana in Bazar-i Kalan since 1996, opened by Bakyt Medetbek uulu, run by his son Askar since 2017. Green tea, plov on Thursday and Friday from 12:00 until the kazan is empty, samsa from our own tandyr. Family room upstairs. The vine over the courtyard was planted the year we opened.
